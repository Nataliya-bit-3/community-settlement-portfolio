How a case actually moves

4 stages

Settlement work isn't one-off help — it's a documented process. Step through the stages below.

Intake

Meeting the client — in person or virtually — to understand their situation, and opening a case record. First point of contact sets the tone for whether a newcomer trusts the system enough to keep coming back.

What this looked like on the ground

  • Coordinated settlement services for ~600 newcomers through in-person and virtual meetings — assessing needs, documenting information, and connecting clients to community, education, government, and employment resources.
  • Maintained client records in OCMS with attention to accuracy, confidentiality, and reporting requirements — intake notes, case notes, referrals, and follow-up activity.
  • Conducted intake interviews and needs assessments to identify barriers to educational access — language, housing, healthcare — and facilitated connections to relevant programs.
  • Delivered dozens of group sessions on Ontario housing, government resources, education systems, and community services — translating complex information into guidance people could actually act on.
  • Created step-by-step guides that simplified government and institutional processes for clients across a range of language and literacy levels.
